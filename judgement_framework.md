
# Judgment Framework

| AI Decision | AI Responsibility | Human Responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| Category | Suggest feedback category | Verify incorrect categories |
| Sentiment | Identify positive, negative or neutral feedback | Review unclear feedback |
| Urgency | Flag possible health-related complaints | Warden checks every urgent complaint |
| Summary | Create a short summary | Verify important details |
| Manual Review | Identify invalid or empty feedback | Manually investigate these comments |

## Human Oversight

The warden must read every comment in urgent.csv
and decide the appropriate action.

AI classifications are suggestions, not final decisions.
